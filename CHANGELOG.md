## [v1.2.0-beta.1] - 2026-11-22

Build: 3
Title: Pokelet 1.2.0 Beta 2

### Added

- Pokelet Studio brings the live preview, character picker and settings into one resizable window.
- Presenter Arm adds a cartoon glove from the bottom of the display while keeping the Mac pointer visible.
- Click sounds can play over identifiable interactive controls. Choose a built-in sound or import a short clip.

### Changed

- Click effects follow primary, secondary and rapid clicks. Choose Companion, Click-only or Replace in Studio.
- Fresh and reset settings use Option–Command–P. Upgrades keep the saved shortcut and settings.

## [v1.1.0-beta.1] - 2026-11-20

Build: 2
Title: Pokelet 1.1.0 Beta 1

### Added

- Choose from four original pointer characters with custom colours, click animations and optional sounds.
- Companion mode keeps the Mac pointer visible. Ordinary pointer use needs neither Accessibility nor Input Monitoring.
