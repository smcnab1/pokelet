# Code of conduct

## Community expectations

Communicate respectfully and constructively. Discuss the work, credit the people who made it and leave room for disagreement. Harassment, discrimination, threats and sharing someone else’s private information without permission are unacceptable. These expectations apply to Issues, Discussions, pull requests, reviews and other project participation.

## Report a concern

Open the menu next to the inappropriate content and choose **Report content** if available. GitHub may offer **Report to repository admins** or **Report abuse to GitHub Support**, depending on the content and your access. Follow [GitHub’s reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

If the concern involves the maintainer or you cannot use **Report content**, you can report it directly through [GitHub Support’s abuse form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse?category=report-abuse&report=other&report_type=unspecified). Include the relevant GitHub links and enough context to explain what happened.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will review reports sent to the repository and handle them as discreetly as reasonably possible. Depending on the severity and frequency, the maintainer may request corrections, issue a warning, remove inappropriate content, or restrict participation or block accounts for serious or repeated misconduct. The maintainer may escalate platform-wide violations to GitHub Support.
