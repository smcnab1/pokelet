Pokelet

Privacy

Pokelet runs on your Mac without an account. It sends no usage reports and creates no analytics installation identifier.

What stays on your Mac

Pointer effects use mouse events in memory. Preferences, your shortcut and imported sounds stay on your Mac. Pokelet does not collect screen contents.

Updates and feedback

When you check for updates, or enable background checks, Sparkle contacts GitHub for your installed channel. GitHub receives normal request data including your IP address and the app and updater versions. Sparkle’s optional system profile is disabled.

Reporting a problem opens an editable GitHub form. You choose whether to submit it.

This website

The cursor demo stores preferences in your browser when available. The download link uses GitHub’s public release API. Listing badges load from MacNative, Product Hunt and AlternativeTo. Those providers receive normal browser requests. This website does not use TelemetryDeck.

See the full privacy policy or contact Sam McNab with a privacy question.
